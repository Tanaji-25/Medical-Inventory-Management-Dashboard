# 💊 Medical Inventory Management Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze medical sales, inventory movement, profitability, and product return risks.

## 1. Project Title / Headline

### 💊 MedCure: Medical Inventory & Sales Analytics Dashboard

A dynamic Power BI dashboard providing an executive-level view of sales performance, profitability, product movement, and inventory returns.

## 2. Short Description / Purpose

The MedCure Dashboard helps management monitor overall financial performance and identify major revenue drivers and potential inventory risks.

The dashboard focuses on sales trends, costs, profitability, top-selling medicines, department contribution, and product returns.

## 3. Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using:

- 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Data visualization and dashboard development.
- 📂 **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation.
- 🧠 **DAX** – KPI and business metric calculations.
- 📅 **Data Modeling** – Calendar table and relationships for time-based analysis.
- 📁 **Microsoft Excel** – Source dataset.

## 4. Data Source

**Source:** Cleaned medical inventory and sales dataset in Excel.

The dataset contains information about:

- Medical products and Drug Names
- Sales and Costs
- Product Returns
- Departments and Specialisations
- Patients
- Formulations and Categories
- Transaction Dates
- Profit and Profit Margin

## 5. Features / Highlights

### • Business Problem

MedCure lacked a consolidated view of financial performance and product movement, making it difficult to identify revenue drivers and inventory risks.

### • Goal of the Dashboard

To provide an interactive management dashboard that helps users monitor:

- Sales and profitability
- Product returns
- Top-selling medicines
- Department performance
- Monthly sales trends

### • Key Visuals

- **KPI Cards** – Total Net Sales, Gross Margin %, Return Value, and Return Rate.
- **Sales Trend** – Monthly sales performance and trends.
- **Sales vs Cost** – Comparison of sales and costs over time.
- **Top 10 Medicines** – Highest-selling medicines by Final Sales.
- **Department Contribution** – Sales contribution by department.
- **Return Ratio** – Monthly financial impact of product returns.
- **Interactive Slicers** – Filter analysis by Year, Month, Department, Drug Name, and other dimensions.

### • Business Impact & Insights

The dashboard helps management identify high-value medicines, monitor profitability, understand department contribution, and detect potential inventory risks caused by product returns.
